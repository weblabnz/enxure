FAQ

Questions people ask first

Is it really free?

Yes. Invoices, quotes, clients, manual payments, PDF export, expenses, backups and two-factor auth all work with no license key. Nine specific extras, such as online payments, recurring billing and Xero sync, need a paid license.

Where does my data live?

In your own MySQL database and Docker volumes, on your own server. Nothing is sent to us.

What do I need to run it?

A server with Docker and Docker Compose. PHP, MySQL and nginx run as one stack, with no Node build step.

Can I get my data out or move servers?

Yes. Scoped backups, CSV exports for invoices, clients and expenses, and a guided server-migration path are built in.

Does it work with Xero?

Yes, as a licensed extra: invoices, voids, clients and payments sync both ways with your Xero organisation.

What does the AGPL-3.0 license mean for me?

You can run, read and modify it freely. If you offer a modified version to others over a network, you must share your changes under the same license.
